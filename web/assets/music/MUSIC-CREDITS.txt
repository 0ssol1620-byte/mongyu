MONGYU R89 soundtrack

All 15 recordings in manifest.json are project-original compositions. No NOM recordings, melodies, or third-party samples are included.

Night Corners: tools/compose_night_score.py
Ten map arrangements and four boss arrangements: tools/compose_map_scores.py
Melodic foundations: tools/compose_chapter_scores.py

NumPy synthesis, FFmpeg/libmp3lame encoding. Marimba, electric piano, glass, mallet, water keys, resonant pulse, strings and bells are synthesized, not sampled. Each file is pinned by SHA-256. Runtime volume, looping and crossfades apply. Project ownership applies; no third-party license is claimed for original work.

The retained CC-BY-4.0.txt concerns recordings distributed in older versions, absent from this build.

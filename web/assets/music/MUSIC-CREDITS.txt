MONGYU R82 soundtrack

All 12 recordings in manifest.json are original procedural compositions for MONGYU. They contain no NOM recordings or third-party samples.

Night Corners: tools/compose_night_score.py
Ten chapter themes and Heart Circuit: tools/compose_chapter_scores.py
Synthesized using NumPy, encoded with FFmpeg/libmp3lame. Each file is pinned by SHA-256 in manifest.json. Runtime volume, looping and crossfades apply. Composition ownership follows the project; no third-party license is claimed for original work.

Earlier builds shipped Raving Energy (faster) by Kevin MacLeod and Racket and Bang by TeknoAXE under CC BY 4.0. Those recordings are absent from the R82 distribution. The historical CC-BY-4.0.txt is retained for earlier versions only.
